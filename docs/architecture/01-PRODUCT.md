# Product and requirements
## Promise and audience
Make connections between life events, appraisals, emotions, needs, protective strategies, behaviors, relationships and social context visually explorable. Users should understand several plausible explanations and what remains unknown.
Initial audience: adults exploring educational fictional stories. Educators and authors follow later. No clinical treatment, automated diagnosis, employee screening, truth detection, guilt scoring or recovered-memory claims.

## Modes
| Mode | Knowledge boundary | Publication gate |
|---|---|---|
| Fiction | Interior life may be authored; invented events remain fiction | Editorial review |
| Historical | Documented actions, attributed reports, hypotheses | Source and subject-matter review |
| Private reflection | User reports; third-party motives unknown | Private, no automatic publication |
| Meaning | Attributed philosophical or religious interpretation | Named tradition; separate opt-in |

Unconsenting private people must not acquire public psychological profiles. Inference from face/voice, social scraping and behavioral surveillance are outside scope.

## Main journey
Select person → watch behavior → open inner layers → visit related past events → switch perspective → inspect loop → choose alternative response → compare conditional continuations → see evidence and missing information → save progress.

Kamran's first episode targets 10–15 minutes, subject to usability validation. Always permit exit, distress-scene skipping, audio mute and a text equivalent.

## Invariants
- Events, interpretations and consequences are distinct objects.
- Sources cannot be fabricated to fill missing information.
- Multiple explanations coexist, including contextual and non-trauma explanations.
- Responsibility and power differences remain explicit.
- Protective relationships, joy and agency accompany adversity.
- Association is not transformed into causation by animation.
- Branches are authored possibilities, not predicted futures.
- No psychological confidence percentages.
- Viewer choices do not mutate biographies.
- Only immutable reviewed revisions enter the public catalog.
- Revoked stories stop loading; online viewers receive removal notices.

## MVP
One fictional adult story, two perspectives, present/past scenes, two competing explanations for one behavior, one interaction loop, three choices, at least one ambiguous result, evidence drawer, Azerbaijani copy, responsive design, keyboard navigation and reduced motion.
Pre-authored content supports the full journey without AI keys. Uploads, billing, arbitrary person analysis and live 3D generation are later.

## Success
Participants can distinguish fiction from inference, locate an alternative, explain the second perspective and identify a condition under which an alternative response may fail. Do not optimize for agreement with AI, time in distress, disclosure volume or dependency. No streak-loss penalties.

## Commercial proposal
Free curated stories; paid authoring, reviewed collections and institutional workspace tools. Payment changes entitlements, not authorization to private cases. No paid promises to reveal hidden trauma. Pricing, provider, legal jurisdiction and project license are unresolved business choices for S5.
