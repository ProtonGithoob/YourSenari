# YourSenari — ƏKS-SƏDA

## Bir insanın iç dünyasına vizual səyahət
Choose a person, explore their life, inspect a behavior, switch perspective, discover possible emotional and social explanations, and explore alternative responses.

**Status: architecture documented; application not implemented.** The repository was empty when inspected on 2026-11-11. These documents describe a proposed system, not deployed functionality, a clinical diagnosis, or a validated causal model.

First release: a curated fictional story about Kamran, a 38-year-old manager, with an employee perspective and branching scenes. Later releases may add reviewed historical biographies and private self-reflection. Azerbaijani is the initial experience language; engineering documentation is English.
